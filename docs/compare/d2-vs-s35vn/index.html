Property by property

Edge retention

Both rate 6/10 for edge retention. Vanadium: 0.9% vs 3%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

S35VN is tougher: 5/10 against 3/10 for D2, a 2-point gap.

Corrosion resistance

S35VN resists corrosion better: 7/10 against 4/10 for D2, a 3-point gap. Chromium: 12% vs 14%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

S35VN is easier to sharpen: 5/10 against 4/10 for D2, a 1-point gap.
