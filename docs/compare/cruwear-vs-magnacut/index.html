Property by property

Edge retention

Both rate 7/10 for edge retention. Vanadium: 2.4% vs 4%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

MagnaCut is tougher: 8/10 against 7/10 for CruWear, a 1-point gap.

Corrosion resistance

MagnaCut resists corrosion better: 9/10 against 3/10 for CruWear, a 6-point gap. Chromium: 7.5% vs 10.7%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Both rate 5/10 for ease of sharpening.

FAQ

Which is better, CruWear or MagnaCut?

MagnaCut is tougher and resists corrosion better, and the two tie on edge retention and ease of sharpening. The biggest gap is corrosion resistance (9/10 vs 3/10). CruWear does not beat MagnaCut on any rating, so pick it for a specific knife rather than for the steel.

Which is harder, CruWear or MagnaCut?

CruWear is typically run at 60–64 HRC and MagnaCut at 61–64 HRC.

Is CruWear or MagnaCut more rust resistant?

MagnaCut (9/10). CruWear is a powder metallurgy tool steel; MagnaCut is a powder metallurgy stainless steel.
