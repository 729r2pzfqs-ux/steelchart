Property by property

Edge retention

S90V holds an edge longer: 9/10 against 6/10 for S30V, a 3-point gap. Vanadium: 4% vs 9%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

S30V is tougher: 4/10 against 3/10 for S90V, a 1-point gap. Typical hardness: 58–61 vs 59–62 HRC; harder blades trade toughness for edge holding.

Corrosion resistance

Both rate 7/10 for corrosion resistance.

Ease of sharpening

S30V is easier to sharpen: 4/10 against 2/10 for S90V, a 2-point gap.

FAQ

Which is better, S30V or S90V?

S30V is tougher and is easier to sharpen; S90V holds an edge longer. Choose S30V for easy sharpening, S90V for edge retention.

Which is harder, S30V or S90V?

S30V is typically run at 58–61 HRC and S90V at 59–62 HRC.

Is S30V or S90V more rust resistant?

Neither: both rate 7/10. Both are powder metallurgy stainless steels.
