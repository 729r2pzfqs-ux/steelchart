Property by property

Edge retention

ZDP-189 holds an edge longer: 9/10 against 7/10 for SG2 (R2), a 2-point gap. Vanadium: 2% vs 0.1%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

SG2 (R2) is tougher: 4/10 against 2/10 for ZDP-189, a 2-point gap. Typical hardness: 62–64 vs 64–67 HRC; harder blades trade toughness for edge holding.

Corrosion resistance

SG2 (R2) resists corrosion better: 7/10 against 5/10 for ZDP-189, a 2-point gap. Chromium: 15% vs 20%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

SG2 (R2) is easier to sharpen: 4/10 against 2/10 for ZDP-189, a 2-point gap.
