Property by property

Edge retention

D2 holds an edge longer: 6/10 against 5/10 for VG-10, a 1-point gap. Vanadium: 0.9% vs 0.2%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

VG-10 is tougher: 4/10 against 3/10 for D2, a 1-point gap.

Corrosion resistance

VG-10 resists corrosion better: 7/10 against 4/10 for D2, a 3-point gap. Chromium: 12% vs 15%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

VG-10 is easier to sharpen: 6/10 against 4/10 for D2, a 2-point gap.

FAQ

Which is better, D2 or VG-10?

D2 holds an edge longer; VG-10 is tougher, resists corrosion better and is easier to sharpen. Choose D2 for edge retention, VG-10 for corrosion resistance.

Which is harder, D2 or VG-10?

D2 is typically run at 58–61 HRC and VG-10 at 59–61 HRC.

Is D2 or VG-10 more rust resistant?

VG-10 (7/10). D2 is a tool steel; VG-10 is a conventional stainless steel.
