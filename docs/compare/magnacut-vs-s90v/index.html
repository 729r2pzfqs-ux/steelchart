Property by property

Edge retention

S90V holds an edge longer: 9/10 against 7/10 for MagnaCut, a 2-point gap. Vanadium: 4% vs 9%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

MagnaCut is tougher: 8/10 against 3/10 for S90V, a 5-point gap. Typical hardness: 61–64 vs 59–62 HRC; harder blades trade toughness for edge holding.

Corrosion resistance

MagnaCut resists corrosion better: 9/10 against 7/10 for S90V, a 2-point gap. Chromium: 10.7% vs 14%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

MagnaCut is easier to sharpen: 5/10 against 2/10 for S90V, a 3-point gap.
