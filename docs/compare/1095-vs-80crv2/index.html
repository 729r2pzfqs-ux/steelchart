FAQ

Which is better, 1095 or 80CrV2?

80CrV2 is tougher, and the two tie on edge retention, corrosion resistance and ease of sharpening. 1095 does not beat 80CrV2 on any rating, so pick it for a specific knife rather than for the steel.

Which is harder, 1095 or 80CrV2?

1095 is typically run at 56–60 HRC and 80CrV2 at 57–60 HRC.

Is 1095 or 80CrV2 more rust resistant?

Neither: both rate 1/10. Both are carbon steels.
