FAQ

Which is better, S30V or S35VN?

S35VN is tougher and is easier to sharpen, and the two tie on edge retention and corrosion resistance. The biggest gap is toughness (5/10 vs 4/10). The differences are small, so the knife itself matters more than the steel.

Which is harder, S30V or S35VN?

S30V is typically run at 58–61 HRC and S35VN at 58–61 HRC.

Is S30V or S35VN more rust resistant?

Neither: both rate 7/10. Both are powder metallurgy stainless steels.
