Property by property

Edge retention

20CV holds an edge longer: 8/10 against 7/10 for MagnaCut, a 1-point gap.

Toughness

MagnaCut is tougher: 8/10 against 4/10 for 20CV, a 4-point gap. Typical hardness: 59–62 vs 61–64 HRC; harder blades trade toughness for edge holding.

Corrosion resistance

Both rate 9/10 for corrosion resistance. Chromium: 20% vs 10.7%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

MagnaCut is easier to sharpen: 5/10 against 3/10 for 20CV, a 2-point gap.

FAQ

Which is better, 20CV or MagnaCut?

20CV holds an edge longer; MagnaCut is tougher and is easier to sharpen. Choose 20CV for edge retention, MagnaCut for toughness.

Which is harder, 20CV or MagnaCut?

20CV is typically run at 59–62 HRC and MagnaCut at 61–64 HRC.

Is 20CV or MagnaCut more rust resistant?

Neither: both rate 9/10. Both are powder metallurgy stainless steels.
