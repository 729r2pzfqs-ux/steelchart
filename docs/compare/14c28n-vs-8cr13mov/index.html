Property by property

Edge retention

Both rate 3/10 for edge retention. Vanadium: 0% vs 0.1%; more vanadium carbide generally means more wear resistance.

Toughness

14C28N is tougher: 8/10 against 5/10 for 8Cr13MoV, a 3-point gap. Typical hardness: 57–60 vs 56–59 HRC; harder blades trade toughness for edge holding.

Corrosion resistance

14C28N resists corrosion better: 8/10 against 6/10 for 8Cr13MoV, a 2-point gap. Chromium: 14% vs 13.5%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

14C28N is easier to sharpen: 9/10 against 8/10 for 8Cr13MoV, a 1-point gap.
