Property by property

Edge retention

Elmax holds an edge longer: 7/10 against 6/10 for S35VN, a 1-point gap.

Toughness

Both rate 5/10 for toughness. Typical hardness: 60–62 vs 58–61 HRC; harder blades trade toughness for edge holding.

Corrosion resistance

Elmax resists corrosion better: 8/10 against 7/10 for S35VN, a 1-point gap. Chromium: 18% vs 14%, though what matters is the chromium left in solution, not the total.

Ease of sharpening

S35VN is easier to sharpen: 5/10 against 4/10 for Elmax, a 1-point gap.
