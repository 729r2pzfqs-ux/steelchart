Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-15.

What we collect

SteelChart has no accounts, forms or comments and does not ask for personal information. Search runs in your browser; your searches are not sent to us. Your light/dark mode choice is stored in your browser's local storage only.

Third-party services

  • Google AdSense shows advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites and to measure ad performance. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Google Analytics 4 measures page views and general usage (pages visited, approximate location, device type) using cookies set by Google.
  • Ahrefs Web Analytics counts page views and shows which sites and searches send visitors here. It sets no cookies; your browser loads its script from analytics.ahrefs.com, so Ahrefs receives your IP address and browser details, governed by Ahrefs' privacy policy.
  • Retailer links: when you follow a buy link, the retailer (and any affiliate network) may set cookies to attribute a purchase. Their privacy policies apply on their sites.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security and operations.

SteelChart does not set its own tracking cookies. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent mode defaults to denying advertising and analytics storage.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser and use the Google opt-out above. The site works without cookies.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
