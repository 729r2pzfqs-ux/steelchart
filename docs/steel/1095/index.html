How to sharpen 1095

1095 is easy to sharpen (9/10). Aluminium-oxide and water stones work well, and it takes a fine edge quickly; a ceramic rod or strop keeps it going between sharpenings.

FAQ

Is 1095 a good knife steel?

1095 is strongest in ease of sharpening (9/10) and weakest in corrosion resistance (1/10). Its average across the four ratings is 4.5/10, and it sits in the budget price tier.

Does 1095 rust?

Yes. 1095 rates 1/10 and will rust or patina without care; dry it after use and keep a thin coat of oil on it.

Is 1095 hard to sharpen?

1095 is easy to sharpen (9/10). Aluminium-oxide and water stones work well, and it takes a fine edge quickly; a ceramic rod or strop keeps it going between sharpenings.

What hardness is 1095?

Knives in 1095 are usually hardened to 56–60 HRC. Makers can run it outside that range; harder means better edge retention but lower toughness.
