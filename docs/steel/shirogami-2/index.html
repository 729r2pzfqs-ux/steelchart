How to sharpen Shirogami #2

Shirogami #2 is easy to sharpen (10/10). Aluminium-oxide and water stones work well, and it takes a fine edge quickly; a ceramic rod or strop keeps it going between sharpenings.

FAQ

Is Shirogami #2 a good knife steel?

Shirogami #2 is strongest in ease of sharpening (10/10) and weakest in corrosion resistance (1/10). Its average across the four ratings is 5.2/10, and it sits in the mid-range price tier.

Does Shirogami #2 rust?

Yes. Shirogami #2 rates 1/10 and will rust or patina without care; dry it after use and keep a thin coat of oil on it.

Is Shirogami #2 hard to sharpen?

Shirogami #2 is easy to sharpen (10/10). Aluminium-oxide and water stones work well, and it takes a fine edge quickly; a ceramic rod or strop keeps it going between sharpenings.

What hardness is Shirogami #2?

Knives in Shirogami #2 are usually hardened to 61–64 HRC. Makers can run it outside that range; harder means better edge retention but lower toughness.
