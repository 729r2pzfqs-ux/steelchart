What makes 440C behave the way it does

440C contains 1.05% carbon, enough to reach typical knife hardness; 17% chromium for corrosion resistance.

Molybdenum (0.75%) improves hardenability and pitting resistance.

Large primary chromium carbides limit toughness. Mn, Si, Ni listed as maximums.

How to sharpen 440C

440C is moderately easy to sharpen (6/10). Water stones and ceramics work; diamond plates speed up reprofiling or repairing a damaged edge.

FAQ

Is 440C a good knife steel?

440C is strongest in corrosion resistance (7/10) and weakest in toughness (3/10). Its average across the four ratings is 5.0/10, and it sits in the budget price tier.

Does 440C rust?

Not in normal use. 440C rates 7/10 for corrosion resistance; wipe it dry after contact with salt or acidic food.

Is 440C hard to sharpen?

440C is moderately easy to sharpen (6/10). Water stones and ceramics work; diamond plates speed up reprofiling or repairing a damaged edge.

What hardness is 440C?

Knives in 440C are usually hardened to 57–59 HRC. Makers can run it outside that range; harder means better edge retention but lower toughness.
