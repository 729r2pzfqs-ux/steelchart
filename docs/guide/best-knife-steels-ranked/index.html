Best Knife Steels Ranked by Property

The best knife steels ranked by edge retention, toughness, corrosion resistance and ease of sharpening, plus a balanced ranking of 30 steels.

No steel wins everything. Hard carbides that make an edge last also make a blade more brittle and slower to sharpen, and the chromium that stops rust is often what limits wear resistance in simple steels. So the useful question is "best at what?" Below, the 30 steels on SteelChart are ranked on each property, then on a simple balanced score.

Best for edge retention

  1. S90V 9/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  2. ZDP-189 9/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  3. 20CV 8/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  4. CPM M4 8/10 · high-speed powder steels
  5. HAP40 8/10 · high-speed powder steels
  6. M390 8/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  7. CruWear 7/10 · powder metallurgy tool steels
  8. Elmax 7/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels

The top of this list is dominated by high-vanadium powder steels and very hard high-speed steels. They hold an edge longest in slicing tests but are the slowest to sharpen.

Best for toughness

  1. AEB-L 9/10 · stainless steels
  2. CPM 3V 9/10 · powder metallurgy tool steels
  3. 14C28N 8/10 · stainless steels
  4. 80CrV2 8/10 · carbon steels
  5. LC200N 8/10 · stainless steels
  6. MagnaCut 8/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  7. Nitro-V 8/10 · stainless steels
  8. 420HC 7/10 · stainless steels

Toughness leaders are low-carbide steels: tool steels such as CPM 3V designed for impact, and fine-grained stainless steels such as AEB-L and 14C28N. Pick from here for choppers, thin kitchen edges that must not chip, or hard use.

Best for corrosion resistance

  1. LC200N 10/10 · stainless steels
  2. 20CV 9/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  3. M390 9/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  4. MagnaCut 9/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  5. 14C28N 8/10 · stainless steels
  6. Elmax 8/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels
  7. N690 8/10 · stainless steels
  8. S45VN 8/10 · powder metallurgy stainless steels

Steels that keep a lot of chromium in solution, or replace some carbon with nitrogen, lead this list. LC200N, at the top, is a common choice for salt-water knives.

Best for ease of sharpening

  1. Shirogami #2 10/10 · carbon steels
  2. 1095 9/10 · carbon steels
  3. 14C28N 9/10 · stainless steels
  4. 420HC 9/10 · stainless steels
  5. 80CrV2 9/10 · carbon steels
  6. AEB-L 9/10 · stainless steels
  7. Nitro-V 9/10 · stainless steels
  8. 8Cr13MoV 8/10 · stainless steels

Simple carbon steels and fine-grained stainless steels sharpen fastest, because they contain few or no very hard vanadium carbides.
