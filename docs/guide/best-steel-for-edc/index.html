Best Steel for EDC Knives

Which knife steels suit everyday carry: steels that balance edge retention and toughness and resist rust, by price tier, with knives that use them.

An everyday-carry knife gets pocket sweat, opens packages and food, and is rarely babied. That favours steels that resist rust, survive the odd twist or tip impact, and still hold a working edge. Here, steels qualify if they rate at least 6/10 for corrosion resistance and 4/10 for toughness; they are then ordered by edge retention plus toughness.

Mid-range

Premium

LC200N (3 edge / 8 tough / 10 corrosion); MagnaCut (7 edge / 8 tough / 9 corrosion); 20CV (8 edge / 4 tough / 9 corrosion); Elmax (7 edge / 5 tough / 8 corrosion).

What the shortlist leaves out

D2, CruWear, CPM 3V and CPM M4 are popular EDC steels too, but they rate 4/10 or lower for corrosion resistance, so they need wiping down and do better in dry climates. Very hard steels such as ZDP-189 hold an edge for a long time but rate low for toughness, so tip and edge chips are more likely with careless use.
